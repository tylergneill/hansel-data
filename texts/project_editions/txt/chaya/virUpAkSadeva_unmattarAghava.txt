ārya, kīdṛśo nideśa āryamiśrāṇām?

nanveṣa sakalakalāvicakṣaṇastuṇḍīramaṇḍalamaṇḍanaṃ marakatapuramalaṃkaroti |

    nūnamasya madhurāṇi subhāṣitānyānandabandhacaritaṃ prabho raghūṇām |
    vayaṃ prayoganipuṇā guṇamātralagnāḥ sāmājikā iha na kiṃ nu ślāghanīyam ||

ārya, mamāpyājñāpaya karaṇīyam |

    avaśyāyasaṃniruddhāṃ punarapyāśvāsitāṃ vasantena |
    ānandayati padminīmanurāgamahāpriyāṃ sūryaḥ ||

kathaṃ kṛtā eva raṅgājīvairbhūmikāḥ?

tasmādvayamapi sajjībhavāmo'nantarakaraṇīyasya |

enaṃ paśyāmi |

    gatirabhasagumbhitai ratnamayānāṃ rucibhiraṅgānām |
    śatamakhaśarāsanāni viracayatīva mahītale hariṇaḥ ||

āryaputra, gṛhyatāṃ jīvagrāhameṣaḥ | enaṃ punardarbhāṅkurairvardhayitvā ayodhyāṃ
neṣyāmi |

vatsa lakṣmaṇa, nihateṣu kharadūṣaṇatriśiraḥpramukheṣu rākṣaseṣu nārhasyapade
utprekṣituṃ pratyūhaṃ kautūhalasya |

    palāyatyāryaputro yathā yathā purato maṇiprabheva |
    tathā tathā majjati mama hṛdayamandhakāre ||

kathamidānīmāryaputro'pi cirayati | sphurati me dakṣiṇaṃ nayanam |

vatsa lakṣmaṇa, tvarasva tvarasvāryaputraṃ paritrātum |

na yuktaṃ saṃśaye upekṣitum |

idānīmalaṃ saṃśayena |

kumāra, tvarasva tvarasva tāvat bhrātaraṃ paritrātum |

na yuktamatra ekāyā mamāvasthānam | yāvaduṭajābhyantaraṃ gacchāmi |

jayatvāryaputraḥ |

eṣa setunibandhātprāgeva samudralaṅghanaṃ viracayya laṅkāmupagato māmā-
śvāsitavān hanumān |

āryaputra kimaparamasmākaṃ kumāralakṣmaṇaḥ priyaṃ karotu |
